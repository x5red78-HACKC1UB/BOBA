Boba Drops for Clubs 🧋

Boba Drops is a Hack Club program where your

members build personal websites and get rewarded

with a grant to buy boba.Run is as a workshop at your

next meeting,no experience needed, and it works for

complete beginners. Clubs are welcome from

anywhere in the world!

Running a workshop

Host a Boba Drops workshop at your club meeting.
Members will build their own personal website using
HTML & CSS, host it on GitHub Pages, and submit it.
Each approved submission earns them $5 for boba.
You can use our workshop guide to run the session, it
walks through everything step by step.


More of a social coder? Your members can also hang out online with other high schoolers building their first websites.



Before you run it...

Check out the requirements & examples page so you know what makes a passing submission, and so you can set expectations with your club members before they start building.

Based in the US and want to submit individually? Individual submissions are here.
